# Delivery checklist

- [ ] Every on-screen claim is visible in the footage or supplied by the client in writing.
- [ ] Phone number / URL checked against the client's current contact details.
- [ ] Safe margins: nothing important in the top 180 px or bottom 320 px of 9:16 (platform UI overlays).
- [ ] Text readable on a phone at arm's length (≥ 64 px at 1080 wide for headlines).
- [ ] Logo has a transparent version for dark and light backgrounds.
- [ ] Export H.264, yuv420p, `-movflags +faststart` for web.
- [ ] Silent or licensed music only.
